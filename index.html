Meet the Director

Jack — Accredited Exercise Physiologist

Jack founded BXP on a simple belief: quality exercise physiology should be accessible to everyone, regardless of funding type, location or circumstance.

As an Accredited Exercise Physiologist, Jack brings genuine clinical depth to every client interaction, working across chronic disease, disability, injury rehabilitation and aged care. He works alongside GPs, specialists, NDIS coordinators, WorkCover insurers and aged care providers to ensure each program is safe, goal-directed and effective.

Sessions are delivered in your home, at a hired gym or pool or via Telehealth. Appointments available Tuesdays 7:00 am – 7:00 pm.
